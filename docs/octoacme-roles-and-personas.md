# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Test Engineers

### Role Summary
QA / Test Engineers validate that work meets acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done before it is released.

### Responsibilities
- Draft and maintain test plans and smoke tests
- Validate acceptance criteria and perform manual QA when needed
- Report defects and quality status
- Confirm release readiness

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers:** collaborate on testability, defect triage, and fixes
- **Product Managers:** clarify acceptance criteria and feature behavior
- **Project Managers:** provide quality status for weekly updates and release decisions

---

## Product Leads / Sponsors

### Role Summary
Product Leads and Sponsors provide business direction and authority for go/no-go decisions and high-level escalations.

### Responsibilities
- Review one-pagers and approve moves from initiation to planning
- Resolve Level 2 and Level 3 escalations
- Confirm alignment with business priorities and funding

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers:** receive escalations, risks, and status reports
- **Product Managers:** agree on priorities and success metrics
- **Developers:** receive trade-off context through the PM and PdM

---

## Release / DevOps Managers

### Role Summary
Release / DevOps Managers own the pipelines and procedures that move changes safely to production.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Schedule deployment windows and coordinate rollbacks
- Run post-deploy verification and support incident response

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers:** support pipeline configuration and deployment issues
- **QA / Test Engineers:** run smoke tests in staging and production
- **Project Managers:** align on release schedules and announcements

---

## Security Champions

### Role Summary
Security Champions embed security practices in delivery and act as the contact for security concerns.

### Responsibilities
- Review security scan results and prioritize remediation
- Advise on secure design and threat risks
- Lead the security incident runbook with Security on-call

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers:** guide fixes for vulnerabilities
- **Project Managers:** are notified of security risks and incidents; track them in the risk register
- **Release / DevOps Managers:** verify security gates in pipelines

---

## UX / Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX / Design Leads ensure solutions are usable and consistent with customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Define usability requirements and produce designs or prototypes
- Validate designs with users and feedback
- Support acceptance of the delivered experience

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers:** shape problem definition and outcomes
- **Developers:** hand off specs and review implementation
- **QA / Test Engineers:** help verify the experience against designs

---

## Support / Customer Success Representatives

### Role Summary
Support / Customer Success Representatives bring the customer voice and prepare customer-facing teams for change.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer feedback and recurring issues
- Prepare support materials ahead of releases
- Relay production issues quickly

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers:** feed insights into prioritization
- **Project Managers:** receive release announcements and incident updates
- **Developers:** escalate reproducible customer-reported defects

---

## Stakeholder Representatives

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Representatives speak for a business group affected by the project and provide inputs and approvals.

### Responsibilities
- Provide requirements, feedback, and approvals
- Review status updates and flag concerns
- Participate in demos and milestone reviews

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers:** main channel for status updates and escalations
- **Product Managers:** negotiate priorities and trade-offs
- **Product Leads / Sponsors:** escalate unresolved concerns

---
